A ninja breaks into a castle: in front of the wall he knocks down ten so the gate opens; inside, he pulls the cord of every kakejiku looking for the secret scroll, which is only in the last one, and climbs the tower. The maps of the five stages from RAM; the title from the ROM's bytes, checked against openMSX; the game in GIF and the five tunes from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1985 - 16 KB cartridge at 0x8000 - MSX - Dump f629b8f7...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The logo and the title come from running the ROM (checked against openMSX), and the maps and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the intro, the title, the controls, the star and the jump, TIME, the lives and the points.
  • The stages: the five maps, the gate, the kakejiku and the scroll, the shrines, the tower and the enemies, in GIF.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: SCORE inside HISCORE, 999,999 that becomes 990,000, the title colour, the scroll in the last kakejiku and tiles made of code.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the BIOS it uses, the interrupt and the loop, the map in RAM, the RAM and the tools.
